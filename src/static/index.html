Environmental factors to account for

  • 1. Space radiation: Crew dose depends on solar activity, trajectory, habitat geometry, and shielding materials; this simplified slider is not a personal dose estimate.
  • 2. Partial gravity: Long-duration human adaptation to Martian gravity is not established; monitor multiple systems using validated mission protocols.
  • 3. Regolith and dust: Dust control and exposure monitoring are mission-design concerns; this dashboard does not estimate an individual's exposure.

Planning priorities (not treatment instructions)

  • 1. Radiation protection: Evaluate shielding and solar-particle-event shelter plans with radiation specialists using validated transport and mission models.
  • 2. Environmental controls: Plan dust containment, air-quality sensing, and life-support monitoring with the mission engineering and medical teams.
  • 3. Crew health monitoring: Use validated wearable and clinical measurements with flight-surgeon oversight; this prototype cannot prescribe countermeasures.
